The rules, as registered

Decisions: Weekly, on the first trading day of each week, from the previous close; fills at that day's close. The MOMENTUM sleeves and the Master can also step out on any day.

Costs: crypto 0.8% per round trip, ETFs 0.1%, T-bills free; both sides of every trade pay. Prices: adjusted closes (dividends and splits included). Idle money: T-bills (BIL).

Signals: 200-day average; momentum = mean of 21/63/126-day returns; volatility over 63 days, target 15%; hysteresis 1 point.

Pass at week 104: MASTER beats the equal-weight hold of all 8 assets after costs, with a smaller worst drawdown.

sleeverule
HOLD50/50 of the category's two assets, bought on day one, never traded again
TRENDweekly: each asset holds its half while its close is above its 200-day average, otherwise that half sits in T-bills (Faber 2007)
MOMENTUMweekly: hold the ONE asset with the best blended 1/3/6-month return if it beats T-bills over the same windows, else T-bills; checked daily for a fast exit (Moskowitz-Ooi-Pedersen 2012; Antonacci 2014)
VOLTARGETweekly: 50/50 basket scaled so its recent volatility is about 15% a year, the rest in T-bills; re-sized only when the size moves by more than 10 points (Moreira-Muir 2017)
ACCOUNTequal average of the TREND, MOMENTUM and VOLTARGET sleeve targets; re-balanced whenever any sleeve changes its target
MASTERweekly: score each category account's own equity curve by its blended 1/3/6-month return; hold the top 2 that beat T-bills, half each, mirroring that account's holdings; a held account that falls below T-bills is dropped the next day; empty seats sit in T-bills

What to expect: in the history replay these rules grew a little slower than holding all eight assets and fell much less in the worst stretch. A few months of live data cannot tell luck from skill; the page will not pretend otherwise.

there is no drawdown kill or governor that can lock the system out forever. Risk control lives inside the rules (trend, momentum and volatility exits), which re-enter on their own. A stale price feed pauses decisions and resumes them automatically when data returns.
